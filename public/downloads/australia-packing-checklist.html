Australia Packing Checklist

Clothing by Season

Summer (December-February)

  • Lightweight, breathable fabrics
  • Swimwear (multiple sets)
  • Sun hat with wide brim
  • Sunglasses with UV protection
  • Light jacket for evenings
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Flip flops/casual sandals

Autumn (March-May)

  • Light to medium-weight layers
  • Long pants/jeans
  • Light sweater or cardigan
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Light rain jacket

Winter (June-August)

  • Warm jacket/coat
  • Thermal underwear/base layers
  • Sweaters/jumpers
  • Long pants/jeans
  • Closed-toe shoes/boots
  • Scarves, gloves, beanie
  • Thermal socks

Spring (September-November)

  • Medium-weight clothing
  • Light layers
  • Rain jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sun protection items

Electronics

  • Universal power adapter
  • Phone charger and portable battery pack
  • Camera and accessories
  • Laptop/tablet charger
  • E-reader or tablet
  • Headphones/earbuds

Travel Documents

  • Passport (with at least 6 months validity)
  • Visa or ETA confirmation
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Flight/train/bus tickets
  • Hotel/accommodation confirmations
  • Driver's license (international if needed)
  • Credit/debit cards
  • Cash (AUD)

Region-Specific Gear

Beach Areas

  • Beach towel
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • Snorkeling gear
  • Waterproof phone case
  • Beach bag

Hiking/Bushwalking

  • Hiking boots with ankle support
  • Moisture-wicking clothing
  • Hat for sun protection
  • Insect repellent
  • First aid kit
  • Water bottle/hydration system
  • Map and compass/GPS

City Exploration

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Small crossbody bag
  • Umbrella (for unexpected rain)
  • Layers for temperature changes

Health & Safety Items

  • Personal medications
  • Basic first aid supplies
  • Insect repellent (especially in tropical areas)
  • Sunscreen (SPF 30+)
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Reusable water bottle

Miscellaneous Essentials

  • Travel pillow (for long flights/drives)
  • Earplugs and eye mask
  • Laundry bag
  • Travel-sized detergent
  • Quick-dry towel
  • Ziplock bags for organization/waterproofing
  • Multi-tool or Swiss Army knife
  • Small lock for luggage/hostels
