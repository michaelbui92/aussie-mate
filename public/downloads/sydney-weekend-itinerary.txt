# Sydney Weekend Itinerary

## Day 1: Iconic Sydney

### Morning
- **9:00 AM**: Sydney Opera House tour
- **11:00 AM**: Circular Quay walk to Harbour Bridge

### Lunch
- **12:30 PM**: Lunch at Opera Bar with harbour views

### Afternoon
- **2:00 PM**: Sydney Harbour Bridge Climb or Pylon Lookout
- **4:00 PM**: Royal Botanic Garden stroll

### Dinner
- **6:30 PM**: Dinner at The Rocks historic area

## Day 2: Beaches & Culture

### Morning
- **9:00 AM**: Bondi to Coogee Coastal Walk
- **11:30 AM**: Coffee at Coogee Pavilion

### Lunch
- **12:30 PM**: Lunch at Coogee Beach cafes

### Afternoon
- **2:00 PM**: Visit Art Gallery of New South Wales
- **4:00 PM**: Shopping at Queen Victoria Building

### Dinner
- **6:30 PM**: Dinner in Surry Hills or Darlinghurst

## Transportation Options
- Opal Card for public transport
- Ferry services from Circular Quay
- Walking distances between attractions

## Estimated Costs (AUD)
- Accommodation: $150-300/night
- Meals: $20-50/meal
- Attractions: $30-70/ticket
- Transport: $10-20/day
