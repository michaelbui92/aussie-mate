# NSW Road Trip Planner

## Route Overview
This guide covers three classic NSW road trip routes:
1. Sydney to Blue Mountains (2-hour drive)
2. Sydney to Hunter Valley (2-hour drive) 
3. Sydney to Byron Bay via Newcastle and Port Macquarie (8-10 hour drive)

## Sydney to Blue Mountains

### Route Highlights
- Great Western Highway or Bells Line of Road
- Scenic stops at Wentworth Falls and Leura
- Blue Mountains attractions: Three Sisters, Scenic World, Jenolan Caves

### Accommodation Options
- Leura: Boutique hotels and B&Bs
- Katoomba: Historic hotels and apartments
- Springwood: Budget-friendly options

### Fuel Stops
- Regular Shell/Caltex stations along route
- Major stops in Penrith and Lithgow

### Scenic Viewpoints
- Echo Point (Three Sisters)
- Govetts Leap Lookout
- Mount Tomah Botanic Garden

## Sydney to Hunter Valley

### Route Highlights
- M1 Pacific Motorway to Hunter Valley
- Wine tasting stops at Pokolbin and Lovedale
- Hunter Valley Gardens and wildlife parks

### Accommodation Options
- Cessnock: Luxury resorts and vineyard stays
- Pokolbin: Wine estate accommodations
- Raymond Terrace: Riverside hotels

### Fuel Stops
- Caltex stations in major towns
- BP and 7-Eleven convenience stores

### Scenic Viewpoints
- Mount View lookout
- Hunter Valley Gardens
- Wandin Valley Falls

## Sydney to Byron Bay (via coast)

### Route Highlights
- Pacific Highway north through Newcastle and Port Macquarie
- Coastal drives with ocean views
- Cape Byron Lighthouse and beach access

### Accommodation Options
- Newcastle: Beachfront apartments and hotels
- Port Macquarie: Waterfront holiday parks
- Byron Bay: Backpackers, boutique hotels, eco-lodges

### Fuel Stops
- Major service stations every 50-100km
- Coles Express and 7-Eleven outlets

### Scenic Viewpoints
- Newcastle Nobbys Headland
- Port Macquarie Breakwall
- Cape Byron Lookout

## General Tips
- Fill up before leaving city areas
- Check road conditions via Live Traffic NSW app
- Allow extra time for scenic routes
- Book accommodations in advance for peak seasons
